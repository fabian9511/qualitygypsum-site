# Quality Gypsum — Blog Featured-Image Concepts (Cinematic)

Cinematic, editorial construction photography — one consistent look across all 37 posts so the blog reads as a single visual set.

## House style (paste into every prompt)
> Cinematic editorial construction photography, photoreal, shot on a full-frame cinema camera, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, soft natural daylight raking through a jobsite with faint volumetric dust in the light beams, subtle film grain, high dynamic range. Cool palette of gypsum white and galvanized steel grey with warm skin tones, punctuated by electric-blue (#00AAE6) accents (plain electric-blue work shirts, plain white hard hats, or a soft blue rim light). Calgary, Alberta. Confident, precise, premium craftsmanship. One clearly defined main subject; if others appear, they are visibly distinct people (different age, build, pose) — never repeated. Realistic anatomy and hands. 16:9, no on-image text, no logos, no watermark, clean composition.

## Negative prompt (paste into every prompt)
> duplicated people, cloned faces, repeated identical person, twins, extra people in background, extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed or fused hands, warped faces, mannequin/plastic skin, garbled text, fake or distorted logos, lettering, signage text, watermark, blurry, low-res, oversaturated.

## Two rules that keep these looking real
1. **No cloned people.** AI tends to duplicate faces and add phantom crew in the background. Each prompt favours a single clear subject; where a second person is needed they're described as visibly different, and the negative prompt strips background clones. Fewer, distinct people = more believable.
2. **Never let the AI draw the logo.** Image generators mangle logos and text every time. So generate with **plain** blue shirts / plain white hard hats (and plain white trucks), then **composite your real Quality Gypsum logo on afterward** (Photoshop, Canva, or Figma — one PNG placed on the hard hat/shirt). That's how the branding ends up looking genuine instead of AI-garbled. Your transparent logo files are already in the site's `public/images` folder.

**How to use:** paste the House style **and** the Negative prompt, then the per-post "Scene." Keep the aspect at 16:9 for blog headers. Generate unbranded, add the real logo in post. Each "Alt text" is the SEO description to attach to the finished image.
